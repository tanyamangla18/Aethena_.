Aethena prototype

Interactive walkthrough. Every simulated feature is labelled in the app.

  1. Home: two categories, Generalised and Rides.
  2. Fake call: Generalised, Fake calls, set a 3 s delay, start.
  3. Book: Rides, Book a ride, pick a vehicle, confirm, start ride.
  4. Detour test: open Demo controls, press Traffic diversion. No alarm is raised.
  5. Real deviation: press Unexplained deviation. Watch check-in, the Are You Safe? call, then contact alert.
  6. Phone off: press it after a deviation to see escalation without a reply.
  7. Pay and review, then open Driver dashboard from the menu to see reconciliation.
  8. Evidence mode: Home > Generalised, or the Safety tab. Start it, then run a ride and trigger Phone off to see segments queue and upload on reconnect.
  9. ETA + offline tracking: start a ride, then use Phone off, Phone dies or Vehicle stalls. Watch the ETA keep updating from the driver and the last-known rider pin appear.
